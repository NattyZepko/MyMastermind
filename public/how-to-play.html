What is Mastermind?

Mastermind is a logic puzzle. You guess a hidden color code, and the game tells you how close you are using two totals: exact matches, and color-only matches.

Example

Secret
Guess
1 2

Meaning: 1 exact match, plus 2 correct colors in wrong slots.

Goal

Crack the secret code by making guesses. After each guess, you get two numbers of feedback:

  • Right color, right place — how many pegs are an exact match.
  • Right color, wrong place — how many additional pegs have the right color but are in the wrong position.

You win when Right color, right place equals the full code length.

Example round

Secret

You don’t see the secret until you win.

Guess
1 right place 2 wrong place

Meaning: 1 peg is an exact match; 2 other colors exist in the secret but in different slots.

What do the two result numbers mean?

The green badge is “right color, right place”. The orange badge is “right color, wrong place”.

Example

Guess
2 1

This does not say which pegs are right — only the totals.

What’s in the secret?

  • The secret has N pegs ("Colors in secret").
  • Each peg is chosen from the palette ("Colors in palette").

The palette colors depend on your settings.

Default palette (sample)

Red Orange Yellow Green Blue Purple Pink Brown

Do colors repeat?

Sometimes. If “Duplicates” is enabled, the secret may include the same color more than once. In “Daily Challenge” mode, duplicates are always allowed.

Example

Allowed

Red appears twice.

Disabled

All colors are unique.

How feedback is calculated

When you submit a guess, the game compares your guess to the secret and computes:

  • Right color, right place: count positions i where guess[i] === secret[i].
  • Total color matches: for each color, take the smaller of the count in the secret and the count in the guess, then sum across colors.
  • Right color, wrong place = Total color matches − Right color, right place.

Example with real colors

Secret
Guess

Exact match: the second peg (Blue).

1 right place 2 wrong place

Total color matches = 3 (Red once + Blue twice). Green doesn’t appear in the secret.

So: wrong place = 3 − 1 = 2.

Can a color be “over-counted” when duplicates exist?

No. A color can only be counted as many times as it appears in the secret.

Example

Secret
Guess
1 0

Only one Red exists in the secret, so the guess can’t earn more than one total Red match.

How to interpret results

  • Feedback does not tell you which specific pegs were right or wrong — only the totals.
  • “Right color, wrong place” never includes pegs already counted as “right place”.
  • If duplicates are allowed, a color can only be credited as many times as it appears in the secret.

Tip: start by finding a guess that increases “right place”, then use controlled swaps to see what changes.

How random is the secret?

Regular games use secure randomness from your browser (Web Crypto). The Daily Challenge is deterministic (everyone gets the same secret for the day).

Game modes (when do you lose?)

  • Zen: no time limit and no guess limit.
  • Limited: you must solve within a maximum number of guesses.
  • Time: you must solve before the timer reaches zero.

No matter the mode: you win as soon as you get all pegs correct.

Examples

Zen

Play freely: no countdown, no guess cap.

Limited (example: 8 guesses)

If you reach the last tick without solving, you lose.

Time (example: 3:00)

Solve before the bar hits zero.
