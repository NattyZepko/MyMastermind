The questions I want to answer, at least.

Who are you?

My name is Natty (Hence the title).
I'm a software developer and the creator of this game. I made this game as a fun project to challenge myself and others, and I made versions of it before with different rules and mechanics, to learn different programming languages and stacks.

I have an idea for your game, can I share it?

Sure! I'd love to hear your feedback. In fact, many existing features are already inspired by user suggestions. You can reach me at Natty.Zepko@gmail.com.

Something isn't working / I found a bug, what do I do?

Also, write to me at: Natty.Zepko@gmail.com. Include as much detail as you can, images and video, too, and I'll look into it!

The colors are hard to distinguish, what can I do?

Under the "Advanced Palette" section in the main menu, you can customize the colors used in the game to better suit your needs. You can also enable the "Show Numbers" option to display numbers inside the colored pegs for easier identification.
