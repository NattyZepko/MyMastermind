Select a color, then fill a peg

  • Tap/click a color in the palette to select it.
  • Then tap/click a peg slot in the guess row to fill it with the selected color.
  • You can also tap/click an already-filled peg to overwrite it with the currently selected color.

Drag a color onto a peg

  • Drag a color from the palette onto a peg slot in the guess row to fill that slot.
  • You can also drag from an existing colored peg in your current guess onto another slot to fill it with that same color.

Remove a color from a peg

  • Desktop: right-click a filled peg to clear it.
  • You can also use the “Clear colors” button to empty the whole guess.
  • On mobile, use “Clear colors” or overwrite pegs (right-click isn’t available).

Reuse a previous guess

  • Tap/click a previous guess in the Guess History to load it into the current guess row.
  • This is useful for making small edits to a past guess instead of re-entering it.

Mobile: resize the guess history

  • Use a two-finger pinch gesture on the Guess History panel to shrink or enlarge history rows.
  • This helps you fit more guesses on screen.

Tip: the app suppresses pull-to-refresh while you’re playing to avoid accidental game resets.

Submit a guess

  • Click “Guess” to submit your current row.
  • Keyboard: press Enter to submit (same as clicking “Guess”).
  • Your guess is added to the Guess History with feedback.
